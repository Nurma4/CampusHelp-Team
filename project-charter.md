# Project Charter – CampusHelp

**Sponsor:** University Administration (simulated)  
**Project Manager:** Student A  
**Team:** Student A, Student B, Student C  
**Duration:** 10 working days  
**Budget baseline:** 45 hours × 50,000 UZS = **2,250,000 UZS**

## Business problem
Student service requests arrive through emails and paper forms, making requests difficult to track, assign, prioritize, and monitor.

## Objective
Plan and demonstrate a centralized web-based portal through which students can submit university service requests and monitor their progress, while administrators can assign requests and manage status.

## Proposed features
1. Student request submission.
2. Request categories and priority levels.
3. Unique request ID and tracking.
4. Administrator assignment.
5. Status updates.
6. Dashboard for open, ongoing, and completed requests.

## Scope
**In scope:** requirements, interface mockups, a demonstrative prototype or documented flow, request submission/tracking/assignment/status concepts, dashboard, test cases, project management records.  
**Out of scope:** real authentication, production hosting, live email/SMS services, production integrations.

## Stakeholders
Students; IT support; university administrators; maintenance staff; library staff; project team; simulated university administration sponsor.

## Constraints and assumptions
- Exactly three students.
- Ten working days.
- No paid external services.
- Prototype functionality may be simulated.
- Team effort is planned as 45 hours.

## Measurable success criteria
1. All six proposed features are represented in the requirements and mockups/prototype.
2. All 12 mandatory Issues are created, assigned, estimated, labeled and linked to milestones.
3. Four milestones, Board/Table/Roadmap views and three progress checkpoints are evidenced.
4. At least three member-authored Pull Requests receive peer review.
5. At least six test cases are recorded and the final status is documented.

## Approval
Sponsor approval is simulated for this academic project. Record team approval and date in the repository.
