# Risk Register and Quality Plan

| ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Owner | Mitigation / response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Member absence | Medium | High | A | Reassign critical tasks, maintain shared files and status notes |
| R2 | Design work delayed | High | Medium | B | Prioritize essential screens and review early |
| R3 | GitHub access problem | Medium | Medium | A | Verify invitations and access on Day 1; keep backup copies |
| R4 | Requirements change | Medium | High | A | Apply formal change control; assess schedule, effort and risk before approval |
| R5 | Prototype defects | Medium | High | C | Test flows before M3 and track defects |
| R6 | Coordination/review delays | Medium | Medium | All | Agree response expectations and keep progress updates |

**Most serious risk:** R4, requirements change, because a late scope change can consume limited time, affect predecessor/successor tasks and reduce time available for testing and closure. R1 can also be severe if a single member owns a critical-path task.

## Quality plan
- Requirements traceability: each prototype/test maps to FR-01 through FR-06.
- Review: each member's document or prototype receives peer feedback.
- Validation: six documented test cases.
- Evidence: screenshots, PRs, Issue links and test results.
- Scope discipline: prototype-only limitations and simulated behaviors are stated.
